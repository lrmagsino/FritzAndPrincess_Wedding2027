# Content guide

## Style

Romantic but clear. Short sentences, warm and respectful, no slang, no invented facts.

## Names

Always "Fritz & Princess" in that order. Use "&", not "and", in the wordmark. In running text, "Fritz and Princess" is fine.

## Guest greeting

"Dear {name}," when `?name=` is present, otherwise "Dear family and friends,". Hero line: "An invitation for {name}".

## Dates

Long: "Friday, April 23, 2027". Short: "April 23, 2027". Location: "Antipolo, Rizal".

## Wedding timeline

1:30 PM Guest Arrival & Seating; 2:00 PM Ceremony Begins; 3:00 PM Ceremony Ends & Photo Session with the Couple; 4:00 PM Travel to Reception Venue; 4:30 PM Cocktail Hour; 5:00 PM Reception Starts; 5:30 PM Early Dinner; 6:30 PM Toasts & Games; 7:15 PM First Dance & Cake Cutting; 7:45 PM Open Dance Floor & Group Photos; 8:15 PM Thank-You Message & Gift Giving; 9:00 PM End of Program.

## Unknown details

Use exactly "To be announced". Label story placeholders "Story to be shared".

## RSVP instructions

"Please confirm your attendance through our RSVP form." Embed the couple's supplied Google Form and provide an external link as a fallback. Do not imply that this site stores RSVP data.

## Wishes and prayers

Use the labels "Name" and "Wishes and Prayers". Explain that new wishes appear publicly as soon as they are sent and may be managed from the browser where they were submitted. Thank guests with: "Thank you for your beautiful wish and prayer for Fritz & Princess."

## Error messages

- Name: "Please enter your name."
- Message: "Please add a wish or prayer."
- Send failure: "Your wish could not be sent just now. Please check your connection and try again."
- Unavailable service: explain that wishes are temporarily unavailable.

## Empty states

Unconfirmed details use "To be announced". If the public wish list is empty, say: "Be the first to leave a wish or prayer for Fritz & Princess." On successful submission, say: "Thank you for sharing your beautiful wish or prayer. It is now visible to everyone."
